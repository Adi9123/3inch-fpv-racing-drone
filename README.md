# 🛸 3-Inch FPV Racing Drone

This repository documents the design and hardware architecture of a **3-inch FPV racing drone** developed for high-precision circuit racing and competitive UAV events.

---

## 🧱 Frame
- **Frame:** Apex 3-inch carbon fiber frame  
- **Wheelbase:** 150 mm  
- **Design intent:** Lightweight and rigid structure optimized for agility and crash resistance

---

## ⚡ Propulsion System
- **Motors:** EMAX ECO II 2004 – 2000 KV brushless motors  
- **Propellers:** Orange 3052 tri-blade  
- **Outcome:** High thrust-to-weight ratio with responsive throttle control

---

## 🧠 Flight Control & Power
- **Flight Controller:** SpeedyBee F405 Mini FC  
- **ESC:** SpeedyBee 35A 4-in-1 ESC  
- **Firmware:** Betaflight  
- **Highlights:**  
  - Stable control on micro-frame  
  - Fine PID tuning capability  
  - Wi-Fi-based configuration

---

## 🎥 FPV System
- **Camera:** RunCam Robin 3  
- **Video Transmitter:** AKK Race Ranger 1.6W  
- **Video Link:** Analog FPV  

---

## 📡 Radio Link
- **Receiver:** ExpressLRS (ELRS)  
- **Transmitter:** RadioMaster Pocket  

---

## 🥽 Ground Station
- **FPV Goggles:** iFlight DVR FPV Goggles  

---

## 🏁 Competition Validation
- **Event:** Technoxian Drone Circuit Racing 2025  
- **Team:** Team Vajra  
- **Achievement:** AIR 2 | World Rank 3
